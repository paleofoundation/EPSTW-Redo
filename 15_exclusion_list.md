# Exclusion List

**Status:** Living document. Authoritative. Check against every draft before delivery.
**Maintainer:** Claude. New exclusions added on Karen's instruction — no questions asked.

## People who do not appear in the book

The following names do not appear anywhere in the manuscript — not in body text, not in footnotes, not in acknowledgments, not in the bibliography — except where a specific carve-out is noted on the entry. If a claim or anecdote traces back to one of these people, it is either rewritten with alternate sourcing or cut. The fact that a source came from one of them is not itself a reason to cite them.

- **Elke Nelson** — updated 2026-04-16. Any content she wrote or offered to the 2012 manuscript is removed from the new book or re-sourced from independent references. Her name may appear *once*, in the final acknowledgments, as a thank-you only — no in-text mention, footnote, bibliography entry, or quoted material.
- **Mat Lalonde** — excluded 2026-04-16, no inclusion in any capacity.
- **Any individual named David Pendergrass** — excluded 2026-04-16, no inclusion in any capacity.

If Karen names additional people for exclusion, they get added here with the date, without comment or explanation.

## Sources and frameworks that do not appear

- **Graham Hancock** — the book does not cite him, reference him, or engage with his catastrophist framings. Younger Dryas material is sourced from peer-reviewed literature (Firestone 2007, Kennett, Wittke, the Comet Research Group, and the paleoclimate literature more broadly).
- **The "Pendergrass Score" or "Pendergrass Scoring Method"** — the four-axis survivability framework appears in the book under a descriptive name, not an eponymous one. The 2016 essay's Pendergrass Scoring Method language is archival only; it does not survive into the book.
- **The Paleo Approved certification documentation** — the book does not advocate for, reproduce, or promote the certification program.

---

## How this list is used

1. Before any chapter draft is handed to Karen, Claude searches the draft for every name and source listed above.
2. If a match is found, the passage is revised or cut before delivery.
3. Claude does not ask Karen about any exclusion rationale. The exclusion is the instruction.
4. If an excluded person is the sole source of an otherwise-useful fact, the fact is treated as unsourced and either cut or re-sourced from independent work.
