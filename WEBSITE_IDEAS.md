## 2026-04-16 — Seed dispersal and the evolutionary contract

> When I eat fruits, I still swallow the seeds. Their dispersal is unlikely. I half-ass honor my end of the evolutionary contract, nonetheless. And the thought always bugs me that fruit is the only food besides milk that actually wants to be consumed.

**Origin:** Drafted for the Prologue of *The Sustainability Diet*. Pulled as clunky where placed. Holds its own as a short-form piece on megafaunal fruit syndrome, the seed-dispersal contract, and the anachronism concept. Pairs naturally with Janzen & Martin (1982) on neotropical anachronisms and with the broader "fruit is a message to a mouth that no longer exists" frame.
